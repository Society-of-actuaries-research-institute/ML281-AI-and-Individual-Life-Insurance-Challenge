# AI & Individual Life Insurance Challenge

This repository contains code and supporting materials for selected papers from the [SOA Research Institute’s AI & Individual Life Insurance Challenge](https://www.soa.org/research/opportunities/2025/ai-life-ins-challenge/). The challenge invited participants to apply artificial intelligence (AI) methods to publicly available individual life insurance experience data and explain what actuaries can learn from the results.

Each paper presents a separate approach. Its corresponding folder contains the materials submitted to support reproduction of that paper’s analysis. The paper explains the methods, results, assumptions, and limitations; its appendix identifies the relevant folder in this repository. The folders are not components of a single model or a common software package.

## Challenge papers

| Paper | Author(s) | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Artificial Intelligence: A Practical Guide to Use in Actuarial Assumptions* | Mike McPhee Anderson | An AI agent for mortality experience analysis and assumption development, with tool-based calculations and an audit log. |
| *Mortality Analysis in a Neural Network* | John Koestner | A neural network for mortality analysis and an AI assistant for reviewing model results. |
| *From Detection to Attribution: A Comprehensive AI Framework for Life Insurance Mortality Surveillance* | Victoria Zhang | Mortality trend detection, early warning, prediction, uncertainty assessment, and causal analysis. |
| *Designing Trustworthy AI for Actuarial Mortality Experience Analysis: A Governed, Explainable, and Scalable Framework* | Wen Ruei-Ling, Xiong Chong-An, Horace Pak Hai Lok, and Huang Chiao-Ching | A governed mortality analysis workflow combining predictive modeling, validation, explanations, and AI-generated reporting. |

The approaches and conclusions belong to the respective authors. Consult the associated paper before interpreting or adapting its code or outputs.

## Data and reproduction

The challenge used the SOA Individual Life Experience Committee’s 2012–2019 individual life insurance mortality experience data. The [challenge call](https://www.soa.org/research/opportunities/2025/ai-life-ins-challenge/) links to the experience report, text data, and data dictionary. Download the data from the source and consult the relevant submission folder for any preparation steps, dependencies, configuration, and execution instructions. Requirements and outputs may differ across submissions.

Reproducing published results may depend on the version of the data, software packages, computing environment, random seeds, external services, or other inputs described in the paper and its folder. Review those materials and any documented limitations when comparing a new run with published results.
